Does skipping past winners improve my odds?

No. It makes no difference at all, and it is worth understanding why before you read anything else into this app.

There are — possible ways to choose 6 numbers from 40. Past draws account for — of them. Removing those leaves your chance of matching all six at roughly — — indistinguishable from the — you started with.

More importantly, the machine has no memory. A combination that won in 1994 is exactly as likely to come up on Saturday as one that never has. "Due" numbers and "burnt out" numbers are the gambler's fallacy, and this constraint doesn't escape it.

What the filter does give you is a line nobody has ever won with — which is a fun property, and a genuinely interesting programming problem, but not an advantage.

Where the numbers come from

Lines are drawn using crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographic random source, rather than Math.random.

The check is a set-membership test: each combination is sorted and joined into a key like 3-11-19-24-31-38, so looking one up among thousands is effectively instant. If a generated line matches a past draw it is thrown away and redrawn — the attempt count above shows how often that happened.

Only the six main numbers are compared. The Powerball is drawn independently and not part of the exclusion.
